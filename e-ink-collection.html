Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Important information about file format, navigation, downloads, devices, updates and licensing.

Is this a reMarkable template?

This is a professional linked PDF notebook, not a proprietary reMarkable template file. It can be used on suitable devices and in applications that support PDF files and internal PDF links.

Do I need a separate app?

No separate LioTil app is required. You only need a device or application that can open PDF files. Navigation and link behavior may vary depending on the PDF application.

Are internal links included?

Yes. Each language edition contains 2,684 internal PDF links for navigation between the start page, calendar, project dashboards, meeting pages and note pages.

Which language editions are included?

The package includes both the English Dark Edition and the German Dark Edition. Each edition contains 228 PDF pages.

How do I receive the files?

After purchasing through Gumroad, the product files are available as a digital download. The package also includes README and license information in English and German.

Are product updates included?

Yes. Free product updates within the purchased Version 1.x series are included. Future major versions may be offered separately.

Can I use the notebook on several devices?

Yes. The single-user license allows you to use the notebook on your own devices. The files may not be shared with other people, colleagues or third parties, or distributed for use by multiple users.

Can the notebook be used for business work?

The single-user license may be used for your own professional work. Sharing the files within a company or with multiple users is not included. Business packages with a company logo and license number can be requested separately.

In case of doubt, the license terms included with the purchased product package always apply.
